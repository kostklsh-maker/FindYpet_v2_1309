        FAQ

        Questions

        Does the finder need an app?

        No. The number on the tag works without anything; the QR code opens in the regular camera; tapping works on iPhone XS and newer and on Android phones with NFC.

        Is there a subscription?

        No. You pay once for the tag, and everything that helps find your pet — call, WhatsApp, Telegram, location, Lost mode, a second contact and notes — works without a subscription, forever. FindYpet Care is a separate, optional program with extra services.

        What if the finder doesn't know what NFC is?

        They don't need to. The phone number and the QR code are the main ways; NFC is just an extra shortcut for those who know it.

        Does the tag need charging?

        No. There's no battery: the NFC chip takes power from the phone that touches it, and the number and QR code are printed. The glow recharges by itself from daylight or a lamp.

        Is it waterproof?

        It's fine in the rain and when your pet swims. If the tag ever breaks, we'll replace it for free.

        Will it fit my pet's collar?

        The tag slides onto the collar — straps up to 25 mm wide go through its slots. It's 34 × 40 mm and about 4.5 mm thin, so it doesn't dangle or jingle.

        Where do I get alerts?

        Today — in Telegram, via our bot @YourPetLocatorBot. We're adding WhatsApp and SMS. Even without Telegram, the finder can call you or write to you on WhatsApp.

        Who sees my details?

        Whoever scans the tag sees your pet's name, your first name, your phone and the notes you chose to add. Your address is used only for delivery and is never shown. The finder's location is sent only if they tick “Allow sharing my location”.

        Will my number get spam?

        Your number is on the tag and on your pet's page — that's how the finder reaches you. The page is hidden from search engines and isn't listed anywhere: it opens from the tag's QR code, NFC or link.

        Does it replace the microchip?

        No. The microchip is required by law and remains the last backup. FindYpet adds a fast way to reach you without a vet's scanner.

        What is a spare tag?

        An exact copy of your pet's tag — same print, same page and link. Keep it at home or put it on a harness. With 2 tags or 3 + 1 you decide: a tag for each pet, or spares.

        What is FindYpet Care?

        A monthly program we're preparing: a digital health card from your vet, partner discounts and bonuses. Planned price — 39 ₪ a month per owner, covering all your pets; cancel any time. It isn't live yet; join the waitlist in our bot — no payment.

        How do I pay, and when will I get the tag?

        After you order, we message you to confirm the order and arrange payment. Then we print the tag, write its NFC and ship it to your address. Each step is announced in Telegram — or we call you.

        What if my phone number changes?

        Write to us in the bot — we update your pet's page right away. The old number is printed on the tag itself, so it's best to order a new tag with the new number.

        How do I update my phone or notes?

        In the Telegram bot: /settings. Keep your contacts up to date — outdated contacts are a common reason a found pet doesn't get home.
