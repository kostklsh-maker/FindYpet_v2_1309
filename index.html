      Questions owners ask

      Does the finder need an app?

      No. They can call the number, scan the QR code with the camera, or tap the tag if their phone has NFC.

      Is there a subscription?

      No. You pay once and the finding features work for good. FindYpet Care will be a separate, optional program.

      Does the tag need charging?

      No. There's no battery. The NFC chip is powered by the phone that touches it, and the glow recharges from light.

      Is it waterproof?

      It handles rain and swimming. If it ever breaks, we replace it for free.

      Will it fit my pet's collar?

      Straps up to 25 mm wide go through its slots. The tag is 53 × 40 mm and sits snugly without dangling.

      Who sees my details?

      Anyone who scans the tag sees your pet's name, your first name, your phone and the notes you chose. Your address is never shown, and the finder's location is shared only if they agree.

      Will my number get spam?

      The number is on the tag on purpose. The pet page is hidden from search engines and opens only through the QR code, NFC or its link.

      What if my phone number changes?

      Message the bot to update the page. Because the old number is printed on the tag, we recommend ordering a new tag too.

      Found a pet with a FindYpet tag?

      Thank you. Any of these reaches the owner, and none of them needs an app.

      • Call the numberIt's printed on the tag and works from any phone, even without internet.
      • Scan the QR codePoint your regular camera at it, then open the link.
      • Tap with your phoneNFC opens the same page on iPhone XS and newer and on Android phones with NFC.

      On the pet's page you can call in one tap, send a WhatsApp message, press “Notify the owner” and share your location if you want to. Read the notes too: they may say the pet is scared or needs medicine.
